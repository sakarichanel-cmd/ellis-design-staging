How We Work

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to stage my house and when do you show up? +

For an average-sized home, we are usually finished in 1 day (for larger homes with multiple areas, we request 2 days). Please do not schedule listing photos for the same day as staging. We spend mornings pulling inventory from our warehouse and packing our truck, typically arriving at the property between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM. A contractor lockbox code or garage code works best.

Should I have the house cleaned before or after staging? +

Always have a thorough professional “move-out” clean completed before we stage. Our design team spends hours fine-tuning the placement of furnishings and accessories, and cleaners inadvertently shift items out of alignment when cleaning around them.

Do you put nail holes in our walls? +

Yes—hanging art is a crucial necessity in professional staging. Buyers expect small picture hooks and it will never hold up a sale. If you recently painted, we recommend leaving clearly labeled cans of touch-up paint for the new buyers.

What if my contractors are delayed and I'm not ready on staging day? +

We understand contractor delays happen. Because we hold your staging date and turn down other bookings, we require 7 days' notice to reschedule. We will always do our best to work you in on the next available date.

How do deposits and payments work? +

Once your staging proposal is accepted, our administrator Marci (Marci.Ellisdesignteam@gmail.com) will email an invoice for the 50% deposit to hold your calendar date. You may pay by check or credit card (card fees apply), with the remaining balance due on the day staging is completed.
